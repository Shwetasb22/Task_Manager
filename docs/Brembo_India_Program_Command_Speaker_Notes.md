# Brembo India Program Command - Speaker Notes

These notes accompany the editable kickoff deck. Replace bracketed placeholders with sponsor-approved details. No operating results, launch dates, or targets have been fabricated.

## Slide 1 - Title
Welcome the group. Frame Program Command as a shared operating view for issue ownership, dates, status, evidence, and follow-up. Fill in sponsor, pilot scope, and date.

## Slide 2 - What we need to align on today
Keep the discussion decision-oriented: choose the first pilot boundary, identify data validation ownership, confirm role/access responsibilities, and agree how success will be measured after a baseline is collected.

## Slide 3 - From tracker rows to controlled follow-up
Describe the product flow without claiming measured savings or asserting that the current process is failing. Raw tracker data enters; date rules derive current status; teams follow up; evidence and outcomes are recorded.

## Slide 4 - Evidence, review, closure
Emphasize that an upload attaches evidence. It does not itself close an issue. The authorized review and closure policy must be confirmed for the pilot and production environment.

## Slide 5 - Department and Description
Department is the structured function/team dimension. Description is free text and remains readable text in detail views. Never infer Department from Description or create charts, word clouds, or other visuals from descriptions. Missing Department is shown as Unassigned.

## Slide 6 - Product walkthrough
Insert approved screenshots only. Do not fabricate application screens. Verify that Department and Description are separate and correctly labeled in each screenshot. Crop out personal or sensitive information unless approved.

## Slide 7 - Daily rules
Explain the current thresholds: more than three days until due is Green/Neutral; due today through three days is Yellow/L2 Manager; one to seven days overdue is Red/L1 Owner; more than seven days overdue is Red/L3 HOD. Missing target date is a Data Gap with Neutral escalation. A recorded closed date is Closed/Green.

## Slide 8 - Roles and governance
Describe Admin, Manager, Owner, and platform responsibilities. Database row-level security is the enforcement boundary. Confirm exact closure/reopen permissions with the project owner; do not overstate a policy that has not been approved.

## Slide 9 - Architecture and readiness
Explain the path from manager Excel trackers through the role-aware application to Supabase-authenticated data and private proof storage. Distinguish capabilities implemented in code from scheduled notifications, which are planned.

## Slide 10 - Launch approach
Walk through preparation, configuration, pilot, launch, and review. Do not present the sequence as an approved calendar; insert dates only after owners and readiness gates are agreed.

## Slide 11 - Success and risks
These are candidate measures only. Establish baselines during the pilot, then agree targets. Validate date completeness, aging, review time, and adoption with stakeholders.

## Slide 12 - Decisions and owners
Read each placeholder as a decision to capture. Close by confirming accountable owners and the next checkpoint. Confirm production environment, data readiness, account provisioning, support coverage, and closure policy before announcing a production date.
